Active · primary

Mottled

Mechanistic interpretability · latent dynamics

An interactive latent trajectory explorer for transformer forward passes. Mottled captures the residual stream after every block, projects it, estimates the local manifold, and animates how a prompt moves, turns, and settles as it flows through the model.

It is deliberately not a neuron inspector or an attribution dashboard. The object of study is the dynamics: attractor basins in the late layers, where an A/B pair of prompts separates, what the decode axis looks like when a model starts repeating itself. Real SAE features (fetched, then measured for fit rather than assumed), logit-lens readouts, attention patterns and an exact attn/MLP residual decomposition all hang off a single interchange type, StateTrajectory.

Model-agnostic by construction and verified as such: GPT-2's 2019 design and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (GQA, RoPE, SwiGLU, RMSNorm) land on the same terrain, across a 29×1536 vs 13×768 divide.

Published · iterating

Meth × neurodivergent reward architecture

Computational neuropharmacology

A multi-scale simulation of methamphetamine perturbations in neurodivergent reward and arousal networks, spanning six coupled layers from millisecond dopamine terminal kinetics to months of chronic neuroadaptation: DAT reverse transport and VMAT2 packing, temperature-dependent oxidative toxicity, LC–NE gain control, trial-level reinforcement learning, circadian and sleep modulation, and setpoint drift over a 365-day horizon.

The interesting result is structural: temperature behaves as a bifurcation parameter for neurotoxicity rather than as a linear risk multiplier, and the same drug exposure produces materially different trajectories across neurotypical, ADHD-C and ADHD-I parameter architectures.

The RL layer has since been rebuilt on ANCCR (Jeong et al. 2022) with IRI-scaling per Burke et al. 2026 — learning rate scales with the inter-reward interval instead of sitting as a free parameter, so total conditioning time is invariant to trial frequency. That replaced a fitted knob with a mechanism.

EVT-1

Agent-native trust infrastructure

TLS certificates, but for product claims. A minimal agent-first protocol that gives agents a shared way to agree on what a product is — deterministic canonical URNs — and to verify claims about it via Ed25519-signed trust assertions, without trusting the marketplace that displayed them.

Shopping and procurement agents currently inherit whatever a platform tells them; badges and "verified" labels are unverifiable platform artifacts. EVT-1 moves both problems down a level: identity is derived (normalization → alias resolution → fuzzy-match confidence), and the server stores and serves signatures but is explicitly not a trust root — agents verify locally. It ships an MCP server, so the whole workflow is available as tools to any MCP-compatible agent.

How I work

The same four commitments show up in the interpretability code and the pharmacology code, which is most of why they are one body of work.

01

Honesty is load-bearing

Any projection of a high-dimensional process is lossy. Every surface carries its fidelity and uncertainty machinery from day one — a lossy picture has to say where it lies, inline and unavoidably.

02

One interchange type

Producers and consumers never couple. New capture backends and new renderers meet at a single structure, so a feature is added in one place and works everywhere.

03

Derive, don't fit

A free parameter tuned to match data explains nothing. Replacing a fitted learning rate with an interval-scaling law is worth more than a better curve fit.

04

It must run offline

Synthetic backends keep pace with real ones, so the whole pipeline stays explorable and testable with no model download, no API key, and no network.
